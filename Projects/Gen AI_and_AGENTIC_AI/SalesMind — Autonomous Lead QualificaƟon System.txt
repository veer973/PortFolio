# SalesMind

## Project Type

Agentic AI / Multi-Agent Lead Qualification & Sales Outreach System

## One-Line Description

An agentic AI sales system that researches potential customers, evaluates their business fit, scores and ranks leads, incorporates human approval, and generates personalized sales outreach emails.

## Project Overview

SalesMind is an AI-powered sales qualification system designed to automate the time-consuming process of researching and evaluating potential customers.

The system starts with a CSV containing raw leads and automatically transforms those leads into:

* Researched company profiles
* Lead-quality analysis
* Numerical lead scores
* Ranked prospects
* Human-approved high-value leads
* Personalized sales email drafts

The core workflow is:

Raw Leads → Research → Analysis → Scoring → Ranking → Human Review → Personalized Outreach

The system uses multiple specialized AI agents instead of assigning the entire workflow to a single general-purpose agent.

# Business Problem

Sales teams often receive large lists of potential customers but do not know which companies are worth contacting first.

For each lead, a salesperson may need to:

* Research the company
* Understand its industry
* Estimate company size
* Identify growth signals
* Determine potential business needs
* Evaluate product fit
* Decide whether the lead is worth pursuing
* Write a personalized outreach email

Doing this manually for hundreds of leads is slow and repetitive.

SalesMind automates the research and qualification workflow so salespeople can focus their time on high-value prospects.

# Example Scenario

Suppose a company sells AI software for businesses.

The input file contains:

Company | Website

ABC Corp | abc.com
XYZ Ltd | xyz.com
TechPro | techpro.com

SalesMind researches the companies and may identify information such as:

ABC Corp

* Industry: SaaS
* Employees: 500
* Recent expansion
* Existing AI adoption
* Potential product need: High

The system then evaluates the company and determines that it is a strong prospect.

Another company may have:

* Poor industry fit
* Small team
* No obvious product need

and therefore receive a low qualification score.

# Input

The system accepts a lead dataset such as:

`leads.csv`

Typical input information includes:

* Company name
* Company website

The system enriches the raw input with additional information collected during the research stage.

# Multi-Agent Architecture

SalesMind uses specialized agents with different responsibilities.

## Agent 1 — Data Collection Agent

### Responsibility

Research each company and collect additional information about the lead.

### Input

Raw lead information from the CSV.

### Process

Company
↓
External Research / Search
↓
Company Information
↓
Structured Lead Data

The agent can identify relevant information such as:

* Industry
* Company size
* Business characteristics
* Growth signals
* Technology adoption
* Potential business needs

The goal is to transform incomplete raw lead information into a richer company profile.

## Agent 2 — Lead Analysis Agent

### Responsibility

Determine whether a company is a strong potential customer.

The agent evaluates the collected information and asks:

"Does this company look like a good customer for our product?"

Example:

ABC Corp

→ Strong industry fit
→ Large company
→ Growing rapidly
→ Likely product need

Conclusion:

HIGH potential

Another company:

SmallCompany

→ Wrong industry
→ Small team
→ No obvious need

Conclusion:

LOW potential

The analysis stage converts raw research into a qualitative assessment of lead quality.

## Agent 3 — Lead Scoring Agent

### Responsibility

Convert the lead analysis into a numerical score.

Example:

ABC Corp → 94
TechPro → 87
DataWorks → 84
XYZ Ltd → 61
SmallCompany → 23

The score provides a consistent way to compare leads.

The scoring stage transforms qualitative analysis into a prioritization signal that can be used for ranking.

## Lead Ranking

After scoring, leads are sorted by their qualification score.

Example:

| Rank | Company      | Score |
| ---- | ------------ | ----- |
| 1    | ABC Corp     | 94    |
| 2    | TechPro      | 87    |
| 3    | DataWorks    | 84    |
| 4    | XYZ Ltd      | 61    |
| 5    | SmallCompany | 23    |

This allows sales teams to focus their attention on the highest-value prospects instead of manually investigating every lead.

# Human-in-the-Loop

SalesMind does not automatically send outreach to every lead.

After qualification and ranking, the system identifies the highest-value prospects and pauses for human review.

Example:

Top prospects:

1. ABC Corp
2. TechPro
3. DataWorks

The system asks the salesperson to review the recommendations.

The human can:

* Approve the selected prospects
* Reject prospects
* Provide feedback

Only after approval does the workflow continue to the outreach-generation stage.

This creates a human-in-the-loop architecture where AI performs repetitive analysis while the human retains control over an important business decision.

# Agent 4 — Email Generation Agent

### Responsibility

Generate personalized sales email drafts for approved leads.

Instead of producing generic outreach such as:

"Dear Sir/Madam, We offer AI solutions..."

the agent uses information discovered during the research stage.

Example:

"Hi Rahul,

I noticed ABC Corp has recently expanded its AI operations. Our platform could help your team..."

The generated email is therefore based on the company's researched characteristics rather than being a generic template.

The agent can use:

* Company information
* Industry
* Business signals
* Potential needs
* Lead qualification information
* Other relevant research

to generate personalized outreach.

# Lead Qualification

Lead qualification combines the information collected during company research with the product's ideal-customer requirements.

The system evaluates factors such as:

* Industry fit
* Company size
* Growth signals
* Technology adoption
* Potential business need

The resulting analysis determines whether the company appears to be a strong, moderate, or weak prospect.

# Lead Scoring

The scoring stage converts the qualification analysis into a numerical value.

Example:

ABC Corp → 94
TechPro → 87
DataWorks → 84
XYZ Ltd → 61
SmallCompany → 23

The score provides a simple prioritization mechanism for sales teams.

The portfolio should explain the score as a **lead-prioritization signal**, not as an objective prediction of whether a company will purchase.

# Lead Ranking

Once scores are generated, SalesMind ranks all leads.

The ranking allows the sales team to answer:

"Which companies should we contact first?"

Instead of manually investigating 100 companies, the salesperson can focus on the highest-ranked prospects.

# Human Decision Point

The system intentionally introduces a human checkpoint between lead qualification and outreach generation.

Workflow:

AI Research
↓
AI Analysis
↓
AI Scoring
↓
AI Ranking
↓
Human Review
↓
AI Outreach Generation

This prevents the system from treating every AI-generated recommendation as an automatic business decision.

The human can review the highest-value leads before personalized outreach is generated.

# Personalized Outreach

The Email Generation Agent uses lead research and qualification information to produce context-aware emails.

The goal is to avoid generic sales templates.

Instead, the generated message should reflect:

* The company's characteristics
* Relevant business signals
* Potential product needs
* The reason the company may be a good prospect

This demonstrates a direct connection between upstream agent research and downstream content generation.

# Agentic AI Characteristics

SalesMind demonstrates several characteristics of an agentic workflow:

## Specialized Agents

Different agents perform different tasks:

* Research
* Analysis
* Scoring
* Outreach generation

## Sequential Workflow

The output of one stage becomes the input to the next stage.

Research
→ Analysis
→ Scoring
→ Ranking
→ Human Review
→ Outreach

## Tool Usage

The research stage can use external search/data-collection tools to gather information beyond the original CSV.

## State Passing

Information collected during earlier stages is passed through the workflow so later agents can use the enriched lead information.

## Human-in-the-Loop

A human approval checkpoint is introduced before outreach generation.

## Autonomous Multi-Step Execution

After receiving the lead dataset, the system can execute multiple connected stages without requiring the salesperson to manually perform each intermediate task.
